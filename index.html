Bump & Go

Privacy policy

Last updated 20 November 2026

Bump & Go is a packing checklist and a birth plan for people expecting a baby. This page says what stays on your phone, and what leaves it only if you choose to share.

Who this is

Bump & Go is made by Kirk Bolam. If you have a question about privacy, write to kbolam@me.com.

What stays on your phone

The app keeps these on the phone, in the app’s own storage:

  • Your baby’s name, and whether you have said girl, boy, or that you are keeping it a surprise.
  • The colour you picked for the app.
  • Your due date and the day you chose to start packing.
  • Your lists, items, notes, and whether something is needed, owned, or packed.
  • Your birth plan: the questions, your answers, and any questions you added.

The packing reminder is a notification on your phone. It is not sent to anyone else.

There is no Bump & Go account. The app does not use advertising, and it does not track you across other apps or websites.

If you share with someone

Sharing uses the iCloud account already on the phone, through Apple’s CloudKit. Only people you invite can open a share. There is no public link.

A list. The share includes that list: the items, ticks, notes, and categories. It does not include your other lists, your baby’s profile, your due date, or your packing reminder.

A birth plan. The share includes the plan you wrote: the questions, the answers, and the due date so it can appear on the plan. It does not include your checklist, the baby’s name from your profile, or your packing reminder. A name you typed into the plan, such as your own or a birth partner’s, is part of that plan.

You choose whether the other person can only look, or can also make changes. You can stop sharing at any time. Your copy stays on your phone. They keep what they already had, and it stops updating with yours.

Apple hosts this in iCloud. Bump & Go does not run a separate server, and the maker of the app cannot read your private iCloud data.

Children

Bump & Go is for adults who are expecting a baby. It is not made for children, and it does not knowingly collect information from a child.

Not medical advice

The checklist and the birth plan are a guide you write for yourself. They are not medical advice. Your maternity unit’s own advice comes first.
