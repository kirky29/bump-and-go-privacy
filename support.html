Bump & Go

Support

Last updated 21 November 2026

Bump & Go is a packing checklist and a birth plan for people expecting a baby. If something is not working, or you have a question about the app, write to kbolam@me.com.

What helps a reply

A short note is enough. If the app misbehaved, these details make it easier to see what happened:

  • What you were trying to do, and what the app did instead.
  • Your iPhone model and the iOS version, from Settings, General, About.
  • Whether you were sharing a list or a birth plan with someone else.

Sharing

Both people need Bump & Go, and an iCloud sign-in on each phone. You invite them from the app. There is no public link.

If a change does not show straight away, open the app on the other phone and leave it in the foreground for a moment.

The packing reminder

The reminder is a notification on your own phone. If it never arrives, open the app, go to Notifications, and turn reminders on. If you have already said no, iOS keeps them off until you allow them in Settings, Notifications, Bump & Go.

Not medical advice

The checklist and the birth plan are a guide you write for yourself. They are not medical advice. Your maternity unit’s own advice comes first.

Privacy

What the app keeps, and what a share includes, is on the privacy policy.
